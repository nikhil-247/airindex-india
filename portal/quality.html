Data Quality & Audit

Quality controls are part of the public measurement record. The demonstration layer exposes acceptance, rejection, anomaly and lead-time coverage indicators.

Six-stage quality framework
01

Validate

Schema, route, fare, date and currency rules.

02

Deduplicate

Repeated observations identified.

03

Freshness

Recency contributes to quality.

04

Anomaly

Route-relative deviation flags.

05

Gate

Measurement readiness checks.

06

Audit

Source-to-release traceability.

Lead-time coverage
Interpretation: flagged observations are retained for audit and handled according to the published release rule; a quality flag does not automatically mean deletion.
