About AirIndex India

AirIndex India is a prototype public statistical information system for studying airfare movement through transparent, reproducible measurement.

Purpose

The platform converts permitted airfare observations into an experimental index and supporting indicators that explain route, carrier, airport and booking-horizon movement.

The project is designed for research, hackathon demonstration and future authorized integration. It is not currently an official Government of India portal.

Core principle

We are not comparing fares. We are measuring airfare movement.
