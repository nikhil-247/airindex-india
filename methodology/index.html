Methodology

From raw airfare observation to APIx.

AirIndex keeps the measurement chain explicit: validate and score observations, create comparable route inputs, calculate a Jévons elementary index, then aggregate with fixed route weights.

01

Collect

Use permitted observations with source metadata.

02

Validate

Check route, carrier, fare and timing fields.

03

QC

Deduplicate, score freshness and flag anomalies.

04

Stratify

Separate booking-horizon buckets T+1 through T+45.

05

Elementary index

Use matched carrier price relatives and a geometric mean.

06

Aggregate

Combine route indices with fixed weights summing to one.

Jévons elementary index
Iᵣ = 100 × exp[(1/n) × Σ ln(Pₜ / P₀)]

The calculation matches common carrier keys between the reference and current route price sets and requires a minimum observation count.

Weighted AirIndex
APIx = Σᵣ wᵣ × Iᵣ, Σ wᵣ = 1

Each route contributes according to its declared basket weight; the prototype replay contains three routes.

Audit trail

Source → Observation → Quality Check → Standardization → Representative Price → Weight → APIx

Interpretation boundary: this is an experimental prototype. Replay values are not live national market statistics and are not an official CPI measure.
